# Cyber Security Policies Basics

A well-defined cybersecurity policy provides clear guidance to protect an organization's digital assets, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.

---

## 1. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
* **Purpose:** Defines the rules and constraints that employees must follow when using organizational hardware, networks, and internet resources.
* **Key Guidelines:**
  * Company equipment must only be used for authorized business operations.
  * Users must not download unapproved software or visit unsafe external websites.
  * Unauthorized distribution of confidential company data is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Password Security Policy
* **Purpose:** Establishes standards for creating, managing, and maintaining strong credentials to block unauthorized authentication attempts.
* **Key Guidelines:**
  * Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  * Passwords must be updated periodically and never reused across different platforms.
  * Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all accounts containing sensitive access privileges.

## 3. Access Control Policy
* **Purpose:** Restricts user access to company files and applications based on the principle of least privilege.
* **Key Guidelines:**
  * Access rights are granted strictly based on an individual's explicit job role and business needs.
  * Regular access review audits are performed to remove permissions from inactive or offboarded users.
  * Shared or generic login credentials are strictly prohibited across network assets.

## 4. Incident Response Policy
* **Purpose:** Outlines the exact operational framework for detecting, isolating, reporting, and recovering from a live cybersecurity breach.
* **Key Guidelines:**
  * **Identification:** Instantly spot and log any unusual network behavior or potential malware infections.
  * **Containment:** Disconnect affected hardware or cloud infrastructure immediately to limit further structural exposure.
  * **Reporting:** Document and report the breach timeline to security teams and regulatory management channels.
